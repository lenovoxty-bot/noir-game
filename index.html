Summary: NOIR: The Mind Game is an offline-capable mystery and logic puzzle game. We do not require account registration, and we do not operate a private server to harvest your personal records. 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how [w,a creation studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application NOIR: The Mind Game (the "App") available on the Google Play Store.

By downloading, accessing, or playing NOIR: The Mind Game, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please refrain from using the App.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The core gameplay of NOIR: The Mind Game operates offline. We store your game state locally on your device using Android's local preferences storage (SharedPreferences). This data includes:

  • Game Progression: Completed case IDs, chapters unlocked, and star ratings (1 to 3 stars).
  • Investigation Statistics: Time taken, attempt counters, and hints used per case.
  • Reward Counters: Available hint balance and completed rewarded ad slots for level skips.
  • User Preferences: Audio toggles (sound effects, music), vibration haptics toggle, and selected text size.

This information remains strictly on your device and is not transmitted to any private developer servers operated by us. If you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, this locally saved data will be permanently removed.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of NOIR: The Mind Game, the App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process information automatically when ads are requested or rendered. This may include:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) associated with your Android device.
  • Device & System Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile network provider, and general performance diagnostics.
  • IP Address: Used by Google to estimate coarse general location (such as city or country level) and prevent ad fraud.
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Ad impressions, clicks, video completion status for rewarded ads, and ad load diagnostic logs.

Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

Depending on your device settings, consent preferences, and regional laws (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area or CCPA in California):

  • Personalized Ads: Ads tailored to your interests based on your advertising ID and historical activity across other apps and sites.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Ads served based on general contextual signals (such as the app content or coarse region) rather than your personal profile. Non-personalized ads may still use device identifiers strictly for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, and fraud prevention.

4. Third-Party Services and Privacy Links

We do not sell your personal data. However, third-party SDKs integrated into the App operate under their respective privacy policies. You are encouraged to review Google's policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. User Rights and Advertising Choices

You have direct control over advertising tracking and identifiers on your Android device:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID to disconnect past ad history from your device.
  • Delete Advertising ID / Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (on newer Android versions) or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Offline Mode: Core puzzle solving can be played offline without an active internet connection. Connecting to the internet is only required to load new ads for extra hints or level skip rewards.

6. Children's Privacy

NOIR: The Mind Game is a cerebral mystery and detective logic puzzle game designed for general audiences and adult puzzle enthusiasts. The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through third-party advertising SDKs without your consent, please contact us so we can investigate and address the matter.

7. Data Security and Retention

Because we do not operate a remote user account database or cloud back-end for personal identifiers, we do not store your personal identity or passwords on external servers. Local game preferences are secured through standard Android application sandbox isolation. Information gathered by third-party service providers (Google AdMob) is retained in accordance with Google's data retention and security policies.
